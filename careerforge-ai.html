🤝 What is CareerForge AI?

🔒 Privacy & AI

Private by Default.
AI as an Option, Not a Dependency.

CareerForge processes candidate data with a private-by-design engine. It runs fully offline by default — the resume never has to leave the device. When AI assistance is switched on, it plugs into a provider-agnostic gateway: your choice of local or hosted LLM, with no vendor lock-in and graceful degradation if the provider is unreachable.

Privacy & AI Governance

Offline by Default

Core analysis runs locally — no network call, no third-party processing of candidate data unless you explicitly enable AI.

PII Flagged at Source

Email, phone, National Insurance and postcode patterns are flagged during extraction so personal data never leaks downstream.

Pluggable AI Gateway

Local or hosted LLM via an OpenAI-compatible interface — Ollama for zero-cost local inference, or your existing provider.

Graceful Degradation

If the AI provider is unavailable, CareerForge keeps working on its deterministic engine — the app never bricks.
