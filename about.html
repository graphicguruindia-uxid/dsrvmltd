About DSRVM

Built to close the gap between business and technology.

Most organisations do not struggle for lack of ambition. They struggle because what the business needs and what technology delivers never quite meet.

Vinod Dangudubiyyapu has spent more than fifteen years in that gap, across UX and UI design, CMS development, Agile and Waterfall delivery, enterprise migrations and operations leadership. He holds PMP and PSM-I certifications.

He founded DSRVM Limited in Southampton in February 2026 to offer growing organisations what large consultancies rarely do: senior attention, a fixed scope and a plain-spoken way of working. Delivery capability in India keeps costs sensible and adds capacity when a project needs it.

DSRVM is a young company, and we say so. It means every client gets the director's time, and every first engagement is built to earn the next one.
